GOOD TO KNOW

A few little details.

How do I request a quote?

Include your service, event date, venue and number of people in your enquiry. Pricing and appointment length will be agreed before you commit to a booking.

Do you offer hair styling?

Hair styling can be discussed as part of your bridal look or as an addition to occasion makeup. Share your ideas so styling requirements and any additional costs can be confirmed.

What are the deposit and cancellation terms?

Deposit, payment and cancellation terms need to be agreed directly with Manisha before booking. No deposit or payment is collected through this preview.

Should I book a bridal trial?

A trial gives us time to explore your ideas and refine your makeup and hair before the wedding. Bring reference photos, details of your outfit, and any accessories you plan to wear.

Can you work with my preferred makeup style?

Absolutely. Your consultation is the place to share what you love, what feels comfortable, and any inspiration. The look will be tailored to you.

What should I do before my appointment?

Arrive with clean skin and share any allergies or sensitivities in advance. Hair preparation and timing will be discussed once your service is agreed.

Is my selected date automatically confirmed?

No. Your chosen date is a preference. Availability, location, pricing, and the final booking arrangements need to be confirmed directly with the artist.
